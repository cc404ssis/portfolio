Sample 3 of 6 · How to sowUnofficial sample, not published by Kings Seeds

How to sow a row, in six steps.

An animated how-to for Reels, Stories and the Growing Advice pages. Tap a step to jump to it.

  1. 1

    Loosen the soil

    Fork over the bed and break up the clods so roots can run.

  2. 2

    Draw a shallow drill

    Press a stick or the corner of a hoe along the soil to make a groove.

  3. 3

    Sow thinly

    Drop the seed along the groove. The packet gives the spacing.

  4. 4

    Cover and firm

    Draw soil back over the seed and press it flat with your hand.

  5. 5

    Water gently

    A fine rose keeps the seed where you put it.

  6. 6

    Wait for sprouts

    Time to sprout depends on the crop. The packet says how long.

IdeaOne drawing that teaches the whole job in about fifteen seconds.

MadeAn SVG scene timed in code. It ends on sprouts, and with motion paused it shows that final frame.

How I work

    Facebook, Instagram and email newsletters, sent through a review step where the team checks posts and leaves notes.
