How I work

Plan, make, publish, measure.

How I would work with you: four steps, repeated each week.

    A content calendar built around the dates your volunteers and supporters plan for.

  2. Make

    Images and short video in Canva and CapCut. I write the English copy. A fluent reader checks any te reo before it is used.
